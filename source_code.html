How to weave

Pick a thread from the bank, then click a shuttle on the loom.

Laying and stacking

  • An empty shuttle takes the pure pigment.
  • Lay a second pigment on the same shuttle and the two mix: crimson over turmeric gives orange.
  • Moonlight lifts a colour a step paler. Kajjala sinks it a step darker.
  • Confluence lets a shuttle bleed into the four beside it.
  • Unravel empties a shuttle. It costs nothing but the strand is spent.

The wheel

Twelve hues. Mixing two lands you at the hue between them — and mixing two that face each other across the wheel cancels both into grey.
