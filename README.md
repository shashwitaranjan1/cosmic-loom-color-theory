## Overview
**Varna-Vyuha** is an interactive web-based puzzle game centered around the principles of color theory[cite: 1, 2]. Players step into a mythological cosmic setting to master a 12-step RYB color wheel, mixing pigments, balancing warm/cool harmonies, and manipulating light values (tints and shades) across six progressive chapters[cite: 1].

## Controls & How to Play
* **Pick a Thread:** Click a spool from the thread bank at the bottom, or press keys `1` through `6`.
* **Weave:** Click any shuttle cell on the grid (or use arrow keys to navigate and `Enter` or `Space` to lay a thread).
* **Mix & Adjust:** Layering two pigments mixes them on the wheel. Moonlight lifts a color paler, while Kajjala sinks it darker.
* **Check Weave:** Click the "Check Weave" button to test if your puzzle layout satisfies the chapter's objective.
